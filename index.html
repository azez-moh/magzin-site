06 — HOW TO USE

LEARN THE FLICK.

Four pieces, one thumb. The basic move, step by step — from our studio tutorial.

  1. 01

    Start with four pieces.

    Half the set, joined tip to tip into one stick.

  2. 02

    Grip with three fingers, thumb on top.

    Hold it upright and relaxed — the magnets do the holding.

  3. 03

    Push down with your thumb.

    The top piece peels away and swings on the magnet joint.

  4. 04

    Last two fingers on the last piece — and let it drop.

    It falls, clicks back tip to tip, and you're back at the start. Repeat.
